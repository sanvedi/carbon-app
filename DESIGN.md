# Design

Single-page comparison tool, designed for shoppers on phones and desktops. A quiet, readable comparison worksheet: cool pale blue #f2f7f8, white input surface, deep blue ink #183b43, teal action #176775, muted blue text #4a646a and rules #c4d5d9. Self-hosted Nunito Sans; left-aligned headings, two adjacent inputs and results on desktop, stacked on phones. Original source and amount stay beside every figure. Loading, unavailable service, missing evidence and incompatible figures are explicit. No decorative imagery or animation.
